# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Page 1: Welcome & HR Orientation

### 1.1 Welcome to the Team!
Welcome aboard! We are thrilled to have you join us. This onboarding guide is designed to help you navigate your first weeks here smoothly. We believe in setting you up for success from day one.

### 1.2 Your First Day Steps
- **9:00 AM:** Meet with your manager (either in-person or via Zoom) for a casual welcome chat.
- **10:00 AM:** Attend the HR Orientation sync. You will review employment paperwork, basic policies, and benefits enrollment.
- **11:30 AM:** IT Setup and Security Briefing. (See Page 2)
- **1:00 PM:** Virtual/In-person team lunch!
- **2:30 PM:** Block out time to complete mandatory training modules on our learning platform.

### 1.3 HR and Benefits Enrollment
You have 30 days from your start date to enroll in your health, dental, and vision insurance plans. Please log in to the HR portal (Workday) to make your elections or opt-out if you are covered elsewhere. Please also ensure your direct deposit information and tax withholding forms are configured.

## Page 2: Hardware & Software Tools Setup

### 2.1 Hardware Provisioning
Depending on your role and location, you should have received your company laptop. If you are missing an external monitor, dongles, or office supplies, immediately open an IT support ticket using the email: support@company.com.

### 2.2 Core Software Tools
Please install and log into the following essential applications using your company Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials provided by IT:
1. **Google Workspace / Microsoft 365:** For email, calendars, and document collaboration.
2. **Slack:** Our primary real-time communication platform. Please upload a profile picture and add your timezone and working hours to your status.
3. **1Password / Okta:** For password management and secure access to our tools.
4. **Zoom:** For all video conferencing. Please connect your calendar to the Zoom app.

### 2.3 Department-Specific Tools
- **Engineering:** GitHub, Jira, AWS CLI, Docker, and specific IDE setups.
- **Design:** Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud.
- **Sales/Marketing:** Salesforce, Hubspot.

## Page 3: Your First Week Itinerary

### 3.1 Day 2-3: Deep Dives and Shadowing
Your manager will schedule a series of "deep dives" to explain the architecture of your team’s projects, upcoming milestones, and the history of your product area. You will also shadow a peer to observe how daily tasks and code reviews or project approvals are handled.

### 3.2 Day 4: Your First Task
By mid-week, you should be assigned an initial, low-stakes task. For engineers, this might be a simple bug fix or a documentation update to help familiarize you with the deployment pipeline. For other roles, it might be completing a competitive analysis summary or assisting in a current ongoing project.

### 3.3 Day 5: End-of-Week Review
You will have a wrap-up meeting with your manager to discuss:
- How the first week felt
- Any blockers concerning your technical setup
- Goals for the next 30, 60, and 90 days.

## Page 4: Company Culture, Perks & Policies

### 4.1 Core Values
- **Transparency:** We default to open communication. Share drafts early, and admit mistakes freely.
- **Ownership:** We expect everyone to act like an owner. If you see something broken, take the initiative to fix it or report it to the right person.
- **Inclusivity:** We build products for everyone, and our team should reflect that reality.

### 4.2 Company Perks
- Monthly wellness stipend ($100 to be used for gym memberships, wellness apps, etc.)
- Annual learning budget ($1,000 for courses, conferences, books).
- No-meeting Wednesdays to ensure uninterrupted focus time.
- Unlimited PTO (with a mandatory minimum of 15 days taken per year to encourage rest).

### 4.3 Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
We have several ERGs, including Women in Tech, LGBTQ+ Alliance, and BIPOC Networks. We encourage all new employees to explore these groups as a way to find mentorship, community, and support.
