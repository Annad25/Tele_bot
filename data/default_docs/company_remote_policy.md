# Company Remote Work Policy

## Page 1: Core Philosophy and Eligibility

### 1.1 Our Core Philosophy
At our company, we believe that great work can happen anywhere. Our remote work policy is designed to empower our team members to do their best work in the environment that suits them best. We trust our employees to manage their time and deliver results regardless of their physical location. This flexibility aims to improve work-life balance, reduce commute stress, and increase overall productivity.

### 1.2 Eligibility
Remote work is available to all full-time and part-time employees whose responsibilities can be fulfilled remotely. Roles requiring physical presence (e.g., maintaining on-site servers, front-desk reception) may have different arrangements. Employees should discuss their eligibility with their direct managers during their performance reviews or onboarding process.

### 1.3 Temporary vs. Permanent Remote Work
Employees can choose between a hybrid model (partially in-office, partially remote) or a fully permanent remote model. Both options require formal approval from HR and the employee's direct supervisor. Changes to a remote work arrangement must be communicated at least two weeks in advance.

## Page 2: Working Hours & Core Hours

### 2.1 Standard Working Hours
While we offer flexible hours, our standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. We expect employees to complete an average of 40 hours per week unless specified otherwise in their contract.

### 2.2 Core Collaboration Hours
To ensure seamless collaboration across different time zones, we have established "Core Collaboration Hours" from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM EST (Eastern Standard Time) daily. During these 4 hours, all remote employees are expected to be online, responsive, and available for team meetings, pair programming, and synchronous tasks.

### 2.3 Time Tracking and PTO
Exempt employees are not required to track every hour but must track their Paid Time Off (PTO) in the company portal. Non-exempt employees must accurately log their daily hours. We encourage all employees to take their allotted PTO to rest and recharge.

## Page 3: Equipment, Home Office & Security

### 3.1 Company-Provided Equipment
The company provides a standard technology package to every remote employee, which includes:
- A high-performance laptop (MacBook Pro or equivalent PC)
- Two external monitors
- A docking station
- Ergonomic keyboard and mouse
- A high-quality headset with a microphone

### 3.2 Home Office Stipend
To help employees create a comfortable and productive workspace, we offer a one-time home office stipend of $500 upon joining or transitioning to remote work. This can be used for an ergonomic chair, standing desk, internet upgrades, or office supplies.

### 3.3 Information Security
Remote workers must adhere to the same security standards as in-office employees.
- Use the company VPN when accessing internal networks.
- Never use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for company business.
- Lock screens when stepping away from the workspace.
- Follow all Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) protocols.

## Page 4: Communication, Etiquette & Performance

### 4.1 Communication Channels
- **Slack:** For quick queries, daily updates, and casual team chats.
- **Zoom/Meet:** For scheduled meetings, complex discussions, and 1-on-1s.
- **Email:** For official documentation, interactions with clients, and company-wide announcements.
- **Jira/Confluence:** For project tracking and asynchronous documentation.

### 4.2 Virtual Meeting Etiquette
- Please join meetings on time.
- Keep the camera on during 1-on-1s and smaller team meetings to foster connection.
- Mute your microphone when not speaking to minimize background noise.
- Utilize visual backgrounds if preferred for privacy.

### 4.3 Performance Expectations
Remote employees are evaluated based on their output, quality of work, and impact—not on the number of hours they appear to be "online." Regular check-ins with managers will ensure that goals are aligned and any blockers are addressed promptly.
